# Site and situation when choosing a campsite

On a backcountry trip, the site is the ground you sleep on: flat, well drained, at least 60 m from water, and sheltered from wind. The situation is what's around it: how far you are from the trailhead, which direction weather usually comes from, and whether you're near a crossroads of trails where other hikers pass at night.

Leave No Trace: camp on durable surfaces, pack out all garbage, and keep fires small or use a stove.
