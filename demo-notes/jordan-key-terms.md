# Chapter 1 cities: key terms

- City: a large, permanent human settlement.
- Urbanization: the increase in the share of people living in urban areas.
- Site: the physical characteristics of a place.
- Situation: the location of a place relative to other places.
- Why cities grow: cities grow where there is trade, transport and jobs.
- Suburb: a residential area on the edge of a city.
- Population density: the number of people per unit of area.
- Megacity: a city with more than 10 million people.
